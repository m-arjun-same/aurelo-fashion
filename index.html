A two-rail studio, since 2019.

AURELO started in 2019 as a two-rail studio with one rule: never blend in. The rail holds the whole collection — every look hangs where it was cut, and nothing leaves it until it is ready to be worn.

Open since 2019, worn in more than 25 cities, eight looks in the collection. Every piece is cut in the studio, sewn seam by seam, and finished to be worn loud.

Tailoring, styling, alterations and made-to-order. Each one starts the same way: an enquiry, then a conversation about the fit.

Asked, answered.

Anything else? Put it in the enquiry form — the studio answers every enquiry.

How do I order a look?

Choose a look in the collection and press Enquire — the form opens with that look already selected. Send the enquiry and the studio confirms size, fit and lead time before anything is made.

How does sizing work?

Looks come in XS to XL. XS is a close fit, S to M is the regular house cut, L to XL is relaxed. Between sizes? Say so in your enquiry and the studio will advise.

Can a look be altered?

Yes. Alterations and repairs are one of the house services — hems, seams and fit adjustments on AURELO pieces. Choose Alterations & Repairs in the enquiry form.

What does made-to-order mean?

Your piece is made after you enquire, not pulled from a warehouse shelf. Size and fit are confirmed first, and the lead time is confirmed at enquiry.

How does delivery work?

Delivery is arranged at enquiry, with the lead time confirmed before you commit. There are no surprise steps — you know where your look stands from the start.

What is the returns policy?

Returns are accepted on unworn pieces. If you are unsure about fit, enquire first — the studio would rather get it right than take it back.
