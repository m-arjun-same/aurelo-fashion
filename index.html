ABOUT THE HOUSE

NEVER BLEND IN.

AURELO started in 2019 as a two-rail studio label with one rule: never blend in. The statement did the rest — today the look is worn in more than 25 cities.

Every piece is cut in the atelier, sewn seam by seam, and finished to be worn loud. One look is never enough — so we make each one count.

  • Cut sharp. The silhouette comes first, always.
  • Sewn honest. Seams that hold, finishes that last.
  • Finished bold. Colour and cut with no apology.

HOW IT WORKS

ATELIER TO WORN.

  1. STEP 01 / 04 — ATELIER

    Consultation

    It starts on the sketch table and in conversation — the look, the fit, the occasion. Every silhouette is drawn for one thing: presence.

  2. STEP 02 / 04 — CUT

    Fitting

    Every panel is cut sharp, then fitted on you. Sharp is where the statement lives — and the fit is checked before a single seam is sewn.

  3. STEP 03 / 04 — STITCH

    Making

    Sewn seam by seam in the atelier until it holds. Not loose, not lazy — held. The lead time is confirmed at enquiry, before you commit.

  4. STEP 04 / 04 — WORN

    Delivery

    Finished on the street, so the statement rides every single step. Your look is handed over ready to wear — worn loud.

QUESTIONS

ASKED, ANSWERED.

Anything else? Put it in the enquiry form below — the atelier answers every enquiry.

How do I order a look?

Choose a look in the collection and press Enquire — the form opens with that look already selected. Send the enquiry and the atelier confirms size, fit and lead time before anything is made.

How does sizing work?

Looks come in XS–XL. Use the size guide above: XS is a close fit, S–M is the regular house cut, L–XL is relaxed. Between sizes? Say so in your enquiry and the atelier will advise.

Can a look be altered?

Yes. Alterations and repairs are one of the house services — hems, seams and fit adjustments on AURELO pieces. Choose Alterations & Repairs in the enquiry form.

What does made-to-order mean?

Your piece is made after you enquire, not pulled from a warehouse shelf. Size and fit are confirmed first, and the lead time is confirmed at enquiry.

How does delivery work?

Delivery is arranged at enquiry, with the lead time confirmed before you commit. There are no surprise steps — you know where your look stands from the start.

What is the returns policy?

Returns are accepted on unworn pieces. If you are unsure about fit, enquire first — the atelier would rather get it right than take it back.
